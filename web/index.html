      How it works

      From uncertainty to a price you can trade.

      1. 01Market dataCoinMarketCap prices, volatility and sentiment.
      2. 02Probabilistic intelligenceCournot, an AI oracle, gives a chance when it has a matching market.
      3. 03Market pricingThe Sooth AMM on BNB Chain sets the live price of cover.
      4. 04Oracle settlementThe Atlas Oracle price decides the result. Chainlink for BNB.

      • AI risk intelligence

        The risk agent reads CoinMarketCap data and, when it exists, the Cournot chance. Fixed rules turn them into a fair chance.

      • Probabilistic risk pricing

        Each level has a chance. The AMM price is the market's chance, and you can buy at it. The agent writes its own estimate.

      • On-chain risk hedging

        Cover is a share in a market on BNB Chain. Each $1 of cover pays $1 if the price at the deadline is at or below the level.

      • Programmable risk markets

        Each market has a fixed feed, level and end time. Nobody can change them after it opens.

      • Portfolio protection

        Connect a wallet. Size cover from the IWMB, EWZB or BNB you really hold.

      • Automated settlement

        At the deadline, anyone can settle from the oracle price. Nobody votes.

      Details: who does what
      1. Creator

        Opens a market with a fixed feed, level and time.

        terms(market)
      2. Pricer

        Reads CoinMarketCap and Cournot and writes a fair price.

        agent/run.mjs
      3. Trader

        Buys only when the market is far from the fair price.

        tradePositions(...)
      4. Settler

        Reads the oracle after the time and proposes the result. A guardian can veto for a short time.

        settleFromFeed → settle → settlePosition
