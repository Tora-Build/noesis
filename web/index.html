        Details: who does what
        1. Creator

          Opens a market with a fixed feed, level and time.

          terms(market)
        2. Pricer

          Reads CoinMarketCap and Cournot and writes a fair price.

          agent/run.mjs
        3. Risk control

          Reads the signed Atlas price and the Consensus Score, and sets the trade size or stops the trade.

          atlasGate()
        4. Trader

          Buys only when the market is far from the fair price.

          tradePositions(...)
        5. Settler

          Reads the oracle after the time and proposes the result. A guardian can veto for a short time.

          settleFromFeed → settle → settlePosition
