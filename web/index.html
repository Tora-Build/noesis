    How it pays

    1. Creator

      Opens a market with a fixed feed, level and time. The terms are frozen on-chain.

      terms(market)
    2. Pricer

      Reads CoinMarketCap and Cournot. Writes a fair price with every input.

      agent/run.mjs
    3. Trader

      Buys only when the market price is far from the fair price. Fixed rules, capped size.

      tradePositions(...)
    4. Settler

      Reads the oracle after the time and proposes the result. A guardian can veto for a short time. Then anyone finalizes and holders claim.

      settleFromFeed → settle → settlePosition

    hUSD is demo money with no value. Anyone can take 1,000 hUSD a day from its faucet.
